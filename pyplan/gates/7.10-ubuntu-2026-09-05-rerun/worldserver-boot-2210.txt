putting the live containers back by hand with `stopstart.py start` (`Controller.start()`) after

auth and world down until `Controller.start()` put them back" — but that call's output went to
`containers-start.txt`, which the install half's own start at `22:47:56` later overwrote, so this
docker stamp is the only surviving trace of it. Every other row is cited to a committed log
beside it. The reading below does not rest on who started it: the timings that carry it are the
world's own `World Initialized` stamp at `22:11:19.83` and `sweep2.log`'s two.

So the empty reply is a sequencing fact of this run, not a broken console: the sweep asked a
worldserver whose world had not finished loading, `prompted=False` says the console had no prompt
yet, and `sweep_driver2.py` (an 08-28 driver) prints `[OK]` for any return that does not raise.
