8.1d's defect: the fix, the review that refuted it, and the redesign
====================================================================

The defect, from this folder's README: a Dashboard that has seen a restart loop
keeps calling it one after the container comes back. Two fixes were written. The
first cleared the loop verdict outright on a backwards restart count and was
refuted by an adversarial review before it reached any gate; the second, at
bf965bf0, moves the LABEL and leaves the interlock shut until the new run has
outlasted SETTLED_AFTER.

Code: ~/gate81d at bf965bf0. The 'before' run has dashboard.py alone checked out
at 9eb0f63a, the commit before any fix.
Venv: ~/gate81d/pylauncher/.venv-gate (Python 3.11.15).


1. THE DEFECT, on a stand-in container  (dashprobe.py, code at 9eb0f63a)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

A busybox container named dash-probe, put into a real restart loop under
'--restart unless-stopped', then replaced. Chosen over the worldserver on
purpose: what is in question is docker's own bookkeeping across a replacement,
and busybox answers it in forty seconds where a worldserver costs minutes of
map loading.

    'Recreated' - and RestartCount still went 8 -> 0. That single line is what
    refuted the first fix: a manual start resets the count exactly as a
    recreate does, so 'the count went backwards' cannot mean 'somebody fixed
    it'. It had been in the first run's own output, unread.

    server's own bots logging back in and out, not a reading that drifted.
