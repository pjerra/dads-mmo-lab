## What the owner wants

Beside "Add a bot of a chosen class", My Party offers "Add this character": a named existing character joins the party as a bot -- the player's own alt on the same account, a character on another account, or a friend's or family member's character. This is the playerbots module's alt-bot route (`.bot add <name>` / `.playerbots bot add`, with the module's own rules on whose characters may be added: same account, guild mates, and the `AiPlayerbot.AllowGuildBots` / `RandomBotAccount` settings -- read the fork's source on the box before writing the spec, never from memory).

## Measured (2026-09-10 19:54 CEST, an Opus measuring lane on `yulon-ubuntu2`; the record is `pyplan/gates/8.6-altbot-measure-yulon-ubuntu2-2026-09-10/`, nothing on the box created or changed)

- The command is `.playerbots bot add <Name>` (`PlayerbotCommandScript.cpp:36`, `SEC_PLAYER`, `Console::No`); there is no `.bot add`. The master is always the calling session's own player, and the session must be a real one: `PlayerbotMgr.cpp:860-874` refuses without an active session, and `GET_PLAYERBOT_MGR` answers only for a non-bot session (`Script/Playerbots.cpp:84`). Every character online on this box is a bot, so SOAP (`Console::No`) and the deployed relay (`dml_login.lua`, same branch) both produce nothing -- and that is also why T13's `dml_addclass` made nothing.
- The five permission rules, one `if` at `PlayerbotMgr.cpp:101-116`: same account (`AiPlayerbot.AllowAccountBots`, shipped 1), same guild by the master's online guild (`AllowGuildBots`, 1), the addclass pool (no flag), a linked account (`AllowTrustedAccountBots`, 1, a row in `acore_playerbots.playerbots_account_links`), a random bot; failing all five: "Failure: You are not allowed to control bot <Name>". Also `MaxAddedBots = 40`, and before any rule an ONLINE character is refused ("player already logged in", `:685`). No "friends" key exists: friends' and family's characters go through the guild or through `playerbots_account_links`, a two-column table the app could write itself. `BotAutologin = 1` would add every character of the player's own account at login with no picker.
- The picker's sources: `acore_characters.characters(guid, account, name, race, class, gender, level, online)`; guild through `guild_member(guildid, guid)` + `guild.name`; account name in `acore_auth.account`; pool and links in `acore_playerbots` -- three databases, three reads. Counted for two masters on this box: same-account 0, guild 0, addclass pool 500, linked 0, online (refused) 500.
- The reply of `bot add` goes to the master's client (`PSendSysMessage`), never to a SOAP result: a bridge script (`dml_botadd.lua`, three lines like `dml_login.lua:26-27` through ALE's `Player:RunCommand`) must answer over `handler:SendSysMessage()` in its own words, is a sixth `BRIDGE_SCRIPTS` entry, and today needs a world restart to deploy (`reload ale` would reload the owner's `LootPet2.lua` too; not pressed).

## Spec

**What the player gets.** Beside "Add a bot of a chosen class", My Party gets "Add this character…": a list of characters the module would accept for THIS master under the running conf -- own account's other characters, guild mates, linked accounts -- each row `name, level, class, account/guild, why allowed`, online ones greyed with "logged in", and the master's added-bot count against `MaxAddedBots`. A press whispers the bridge's `dml_botadd <master> <name>`; the bridge runs `.playerbots bot add <name>` as the master and answers over the SOAP result with the module's own sentence; the panel polls the group table as `add_bot` does and reports in the app's voice. A second control, "Link an account…", writes a `playerbots_account_links` row (master's account, the other account by name) with a confirmation naming both and the fact that the module treats every character of that account as addable from then on; this is the friends-and-family route the owner asked for, and it is a write, so the ledger gets a row.

**The hard fact the spec is built on:** `bot add` only works from a real, non-bot player session. So the live half needs a client-driven master (the win11 VM's client, `driving-a-wow-client-unattended.md`), and the bridge script must run the command as that player. The unit half: the picker's filter (three reads, the five rules spelled from the source lines above, `online` and `MaxAddedBots` first), the bridge script and its Python seam with the sentence grammar pinned to the shipped Lua as T13 did, `Link an account` with its write and refusal (unknown account name, own account, already linked). The live half: an alt on the same account added; a guild mate added; a linked account's character added; an online character refused; the sixth rule (the addclass pool) already covered by 8.6's existing route. WotLK only; the other trees do not offer the control.

**Not in scope:** cross-faction (unchecked in the module; measure first), `BotAutologin` (a conf change the owner has not asked for), `KeepAltsInGroup`.
